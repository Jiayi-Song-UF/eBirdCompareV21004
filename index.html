How was this threshold selected?

These are fitted agreement scores against eBird, not independent validation. The same eBird reference selected the threshold.

Methods, coverage & data sources

CSSDM uses the mean of five calibrated family-background models from the 20261004 experiment. Their strong/weak labels use family-level Good’s coverage and within-family pseudo-absences, with one shared species output head. For each species, thresholds run from its minimum to maximum predicted value in steps of 0.01 (the exact maximum is also tested). Presence means score > threshold; equality means absence. Select the largest threshold among exact F1 ties. No model is retrained for this viewer.

eBird presence means positive 2023 Status and Trends mean abundance; zero means modeled absence. A 504 m grid pixel inherits the label of the native 3 km eBird pixel containing its center. This does not add spatial detail to eBird. Both layers are masked to Florida; the eBird display includes all 67 counties. CSSDM covers 579,752 cells in 66 counties, with Monroe missing.

F1, precision and recall refer to the original 66-county CSSDM support. For this experiment's threshold fitting, missing eBird values were counted as absence, as requested. On the map, missing values are gray so they are not confused with genuine zero abundance. CSSDM gaps are never treated as absence. The retained species each have positive abundance in at least one native eBird pixel intersecting Florida, checked across all 67 counties.

Many fine cells share one eBird reference label; they are not independent observations. High F1 can reflect widespread presence. The threshold details therefore also show the all-presence baseline. These maps compare two models, not ground-truth occupancy.
